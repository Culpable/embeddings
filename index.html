what we build - AI that connects to the way you already work

Every build starts from your systems, your data and the work your people do every day.

  • Knowledge assistants

    Staff ask questions in plain English and get answers from your own documents, policies and records, with the source attached.

  • Customer chatbots

    Answer customer questions on your site or portal from your real data, and hand over to your team when a person is needed.

  • Workflow automation

    Take the repetitive steps out of quoting, reporting, onboarding and document handling, from start to finish.

  • System integration

    Connect AI to the software you already run, including ERP, CRM, project management and custom-built tools.

  • Agent orchestration

    Coordinate several agents across departments and software suites, so work moves between them with the right checks and approvals.

how we work - Solutions, not slideshows

We don’t stop at advice. We build the system, put it into production, and keep it working.

  1. Discover

    We map the work, the people and the systems involved, and find where AI will help and where it won’t.

    You get: A short list of opportunities, ranked by value and effort

  2. Build

    We build a working version on your real data and test it with the people who will use it.

    You get: A working system your team has already tried

  3. Deploy

    We connect it to your existing software, with the security and access controls your organisation needs.

    You get: A live system inside the tools your team already uses

  4. Operate

    We monitor, support and improve it as your business and the technology change.

    You get: Ongoing support and regular improvements
